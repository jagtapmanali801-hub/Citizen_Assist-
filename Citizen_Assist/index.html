            ABOUT CITIZEN ASSIST

            Making government digital services easier to understand.

            Citizen Assist is a college CEP project built as an independent educational and procedural guidance platform. It helps people discover the right government service, understand the preparation requirements, and reach the correct official portal with confidence.

            Independent platformEnglish • हिंदी • मराठीZero-PII
            Maharashtra Government ServicesGuidance before official portal handoff
            01 • THE IDEA

            Why we created Citizen Assist

            Government services are important, but the digital journey can feel confusing: users may not know which service to choose, what documents to prepare, where to find the official portal, or what to do when an OTP, upload, payment or session issue occurs.

            Citizen Assist adds a simple preparation layer before the government transaction.

            02 • OUR MISSION

            Discover → Understand → Prepare → Verify → Redirect

            Every part of the website follows this journey. We explain the service in simple language, provide a checklist, highlight common mistakes, and then send the user to the verified official destination.

            03 • WHO IT HELPS

            Student • Citizen • Worker • Others

            • Students: scholarships, education and document guidance.
            • Citizens: certificates, identity, family and public services.
            • Workers: labour, welfare and simplified multilingual guidance.
            • Others: access the full service directory without a persona filter.
            04 • WHAT YOU GET

            A complete preparation experience

            🔎 Search & smart matching🗂️ Category & persona filters📄 Required-document guidance🪜 Step-by-step instructions☑️ Interactive checklist📊 Live preparation progress🤖 Citizen Assist chatbot🌐 Official portal handoff
            05 • SAFETY & PRIVACY

            We guide — we don't collect sensitive government information.

            Never enter here

            Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, passwords, OTPs, financial credentials or other sensitive government identifiers.

            Where applications happen

            Actual government applications and submissions happen on the verified official government portal, outside Citizen Assist.

            Why this matters

            The platform is designed as an informational layer, not as a government authority or transaction processor.

            06 • HOW CITIZEN ASSIST WORKS

            Your journey in 7 simple steps

            07 • MAHARASHTRA SERVICE ECOSYSTEM

            Built to connect users with the wider official catalogue

            The official Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar dashboard currently displays 1,212 notified services, 1,083 services available on the portal, and 38 departments. Citizen Assist provides a simpler discovery and preparation layer while the official catalogue remains the source of truth.

            08 • ACCESSIBILITY

            Designed for more people

            English, Hindi and Marathi support, scalable text, high contrast, black-and-white mode, keyboard-friendly navigation and large touch targets help make the interface easier to use.

            09 • TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

            Know who provides the information

            Citizen Assist is an independent college CEP project, not a government website. Government facts and destinations are separated from our guidance, with the official domain and last-checked information shown where available.

            10 • DESIGN REVIEW ACTIONS

            Built around the citizen task

            Find first

            Search and common tasks are placed before decorative statistics.

            Verify before leaving

            Official-domain evidence is shown before the user continues to a government portal.

            AI stays transparent

            The chatbot is an assistive guide, not an official government authority. Users are directed to source-linked guidance and official portals.
